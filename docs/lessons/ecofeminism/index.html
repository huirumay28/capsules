  1. 1973 Chipko begins April 1973: In Mandal village, Garhwal Himalaya, residents led by Chandi Prasad Bhatt confront loggers and embrace trees to stop commercial felling. The Hindi word means 'to stick' or 'to hug.' This tactic spreads across Uttarakhand villages.

Before

The word is late; the politics came first

as a named movement begins in 1974, when Françoise d'Eaubonne published Le Féminisme ou la Mort and coined écoféminisme. The claim that women's oppression and environmental destruction share causes was already live in organizing.

The concept

Linked structures, not metaphor

claims systems that exploit nature and systems that exploit women share mechanisms: labor extraction, land control, decision-making exclusion, toxic exposure.

Fun fact

Chipko's name comes from a real tactic

The Hindi word means 'to stick' or 'to embrace,' and participants literally wrapped their arms around tree trunks so loggers could not swing axes without hitting human bodies first.
