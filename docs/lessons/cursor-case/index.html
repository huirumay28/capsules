A story

Distribution versus the native editor

TechCrunch asked, in 2023, whether a five-person could compete with Microsoft. Truell’s answer was that VS Code’s breadth was also a brake: Microsoft could not change the editor radically without risking the people who already lived there. , being small and specialized, could. That is a classic disruptor paragraph. It may or may not remain true once a product is no longer small. The case is useful because the paragraph is dated. You can watch what happened after.

The strategic object is not a model. Models are rented. The object is the place the programmer’s hands already are, and whether “AI” is a guest in that place or the room itself.

Fun fact

They told TechCrunch they would not clone and sell

In the 11 October 2023 TechCrunch interview, CEO Michael Truell said the “ceiling in the AI coding space is so high” that it was “not possible to just clone the tech and then put great sales on top.” Whether that remains a good description of the category is a 2026 question. The quote is useful because it is dated and attributed: a five-person company, just seeded, explaining why it thought Microsoft’s distribution advantage was not the whole game.
