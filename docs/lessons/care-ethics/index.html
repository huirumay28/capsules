The concept

What care ethics claims

argues that moral life is about interdependence, attentiveness to need, responsibility for concrete others, and maintaining relationships. Not deficient versions of justice reasoning; a distinct moral orientation.

A question

A question to keep asking

When you make a moral judgment, do you ask only about justice and rules, or do you also ask: who will do the care work, whose needs are attended to, what relationships are sustained or broken?
