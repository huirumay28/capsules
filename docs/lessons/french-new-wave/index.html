French New Wave (c. 1958–64): the camera leaves the studio

Truffaut, Godard, Varda as a peer — auteurs, jump cuts, and Paris as a location, not a set.

Before

Critics with a grudge, and a camera that could walk

In the 1950s a set of Paris critics used to pick a fight with their own national cinema. Truffaut’s 1954 essay “Une certaine tendance du cinéma français” attacked what he called the : well-upholstered literary adaptations, studio craft, a cinema that looked finished and felt, to him, dead. The was the other half of the fight. A director, even in Hollywood, could be an author. Hitchcock counted. So did Hawks. The insult to the French studio system was the point.

They were not the first people to want a camera outside. Italian neorealism had already taken the war’s rubble as a set. What was new in Paris was the combination: critics who became directors, a magazine that had already trained an audience, and equipment that made a low budget look like a choice.

The era

1959 is a date. It is not the whole map.

Les Quatre Cents Coups premiered at in 1959. Truffaut, who had been banned from the festival as a critic, arrived as a director. The film follows Antoine Doinel through a Paris that is not a postcard — school, streets, a runaway, a freeze-frame on a beach. It is the date the press needed. It is not the first film in the cluster.

À bout de souffle (1960) is the film people mean when they say “.” Godard and his editor cut inside shots; time skips; the hero talks to the camera as if the fourth wall were optional. The plot is a thin crime story. The event is the grammar.

Agnès Varda is not a cameo in a men’s movement. La Pointe Courte (1955) is often called a film before the name existed. Cléo de 5 à 7 (1962) walks a singer through two real-time hours of Paris while she waits for a medical result. If “” only fits the men, the word is too small — the same problem a later feminism lesson will have with “first wave.”

The same years, elsewhere: in Japan, Oshima, Imamura, Teshigahara and others were breaking studio form; the Japanese phrase nūberu bāgu borrows the French name and then goes its own way. In Britain, Free Cinema’s shorts and manifesto (Anderson, Reisz, Richardson) had already argued for everyday subjects and a camera that did not owe the studio a polished lie. These are contemporaneous arguments, not a seating chart with France at the head.

People

Three directors, not a trinity

François Truffaut

1932–1984

Critic, then director. Les Quatre Cents Coups made the movement a story. He kept coming back to Antoine Doinel. The man who attacked the became, in time, a tradition of his own — which is a later capsule.

Jean-Luc Godard

1930–2022

À bout de souffle, then a career of breaking the toy he had just been given. are the textbook; the longer fact is a director who treated cinema as an argument that could be continued in public.

Agnès Varda

1928–2019

Photographer first, then filmmaker. La Pointe Courte, Cléo, and decades of work after the name had gone cold. If a syllabus puts her in a sidebar, the syllabus is the artifact.

A story

Who gets to be the author

The was a way of watching Hollywood with respect and watching French “quality” with contempt. It gave critics a hero: the director as signature. It also made it easy to miss the editor, the cinematographer, the producer, the actor who is carrying the film. Breathless without Raoul Coutard’s camera, or without Jean Seberg’s face, is a different object. Varda’s career is a useful correction: the author can be a woman who was never on the masthead as a boy critic.

Keep the in mind. Authorship, in this era, is also a question of who can afford to leave the studio, and who is allowed to.

After

New Hollywood, and a cousin who arrives late

Fun fact

The jump cuts were also a budget

Godard and editor Cécile Decugis shortened Breathless by cutting inside shots, not only between them. The jump cut is now taught as a philosophy of time. It was also a way to lose running time without reshooting Paris. Both things can be true: a device can be an idea and a solution to a reel that was too long.
